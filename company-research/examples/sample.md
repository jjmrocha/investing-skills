## TL;DR
Healthcare group in two segments: prescription drugs (Innovative Medicine, $60.4B) and medical
devices (MedTech, $33.8B). FY2025 revenue $94.2B, up 6.0%. Net income $26.8B, EPS $11.03 —
against $14.1B and $5.79 in FY2024, a base depressed by an approximately $5.0B talc charge.
R&D $14.7B, 15.6% of sales. Total debt $49.3B against $20.1B cash; rated AAA/Aaa, both stable.
~138,200 employees. No controlling shareholder; Vanguard ~9.7%, BlackRock ~8.3%, State Street ~5.5%.
Consumer health was separated as Kenvue in 2023. Three wholesalers account for roughly 48% of
gross revenue. Talc litigation returned to the tort system in 2025 after a third failed bankruptcy
resolution; the reserve fell from ~$11.6B to ~$3.4B because it now covers only executed settlements
and defence costs, not comprehensive resolution. STELARA biosimilar erosion cost Innovative
Medicine roughly 1,040bps of growth.

- **Sector:** Pharmaceuticals and medical devices

Hospitals, health systems, surgeons and pharmaceutical wholesalers. The FY2025 10-K discloses that

Currency contributed +0.7% to both segments in FY2025.

## History
Founded 1886 in New Brunswick. Operated for over a century as a three-segment group —
pharmaceuticals, devices, consumer health. **May 2023:** consumer health separated via the Kenvue
IPO, 198,734,444 shares at $22.00, net proceeds $4.2B, with full separation completed later that
year. **2021 onward:** three successive attempts to resolve talc liability through Chapter 11,
all dismissed. **April 2025:** acquired Intra-Cellular Therapies for $14.6B, bringing CAPLYTA.

## Ownership
- **Institutional total:** ~71%
- **Largest holders (13F, Q3 2025):** Vanguard ~9.72% (233.96M shares); BlackRock ~8.29% (199.37M);
  State Street ~5.50% (132.80M)
- **Control structure:** single class of common stock; no controlling shareholder

## Recent developments
- **Jan 2, 2026** — quarterly dividend declared at $1.30/share
- **Jan 2026** — MDL Special Master issued report on causation and asbestos testing methods
- **Apr 2, 2025** — Intra-Cellular Therapies acquisition closed, $14.6B
- **Apr 25, 2025** — S&P affirmed AAA and removed the CreditWatch negative placed in January
  following the Intra-Cellular acquisition
- **Mar 2025** — Texas Bankruptcy Court dismissed the Red River case; J&J declined to appeal and
  returned to litigating talc claims in the tort system

## Business model and competitive position
**Innovative Medicine** earns revenue on patent-protected drugs sold through wholesalers to
hospitals and pharmacies; economics depend on exclusivity periods and are stepped down sharply by
biosimilar entry. STELARA's erosion cost the segment roughly **1,040 basis points** of FY2025
growth — a single product moving segment growth by more than ten points. The 10-K notes at least
two biosimilars pursuing approval for a SIMPONI biosimilar in the US, which it states would likely
result in a significant reduction in future sales.

**MedTech** sells devices into hospital procurement, with growth in FY2025 driven by
electrophysiology, Abiomed and Shockwave in cardiovascular, and wound closure in general surgery.

**Evidence bearing on durability:** R&D at $14.7B, 15.6% of sales. AAA/Aaa ratings with adjusted
leverage generally under 1.0x. Against that: the concentration disclosure above, and a pipeline
that must replace named products on known exclusivity clocks. The 10-K names biosimilar entry as
the mechanism, and quantifies it for STELARA.

## Strategy
Capital went to acquisitions ($14.6B for Intra-Cellular in a single deal), R&D ($14.7B), and
shareholder returns ($5.14/share in dividends paid). The Kenvue separation removed consumer health
entirely, concentrating the group in two segments with different customers and different economics.

## Risks
**Talc litigation — the reserve movement is the story.** At FY2024 year-end the reserve stood at a
present value of approximately **$11.6B** (nominal ~$13.5B), reflecting a proposed prepackaged plan
valued at ~$6.475B present value over 25 years (nominal ~$8.0B, discounted at 4.4%) covering 99.75%
of then-pending suits. After the March 2025 dismissal, the Q4 2025 reserve is approximately
**$3.4B**, roughly a third of it current — and the 10-K states it now comprises previously executed
settlements, litigation defence and other costs. The reserve fell by ~$8B not because exposure fell
but because it stopped representing a comprehensive resolution. Ovarian cancer trials are being
scheduled in state courts through 2026 and beyond.
*Source: FY2025 10-K, Legal Proceedings*

**Biosimilar erosion.** Quantified above for STELARA; flagged prospectively for SIMPONI.

**Customer concentration.** Three wholesalers, ~48% of gross revenue.

**Opioid litigation.** Disclosed as a continuing matter in Legal Proceedings.

## What employees say
Glassdoor 4.1/5 across 13,271 reviews; 80% would recommend to a friend; rating stable over the
prior twelve months. Recurring positives: pay, benefits, work-life balance, development
opportunity. Recurring negatives: pace and burnout risk, and process complexity attributed to
company size.
